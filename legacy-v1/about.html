About ISAP Forum

Establishing a credible professional and academic community connecting Information Systems researchers, practitioners, and policy stakeholders in Ghana and Africa.

Official Mission Statement

Bridging Theory and Practice

The mission of the ISAP Forum is to bridge the gap between theory and practice in the field of Information Systems. We provide a dynamic, collaborative, and inclusive platform where academics, researchers, students, industry professionals, and consultants can connect, share knowledge, debate emerging trends, shape national policy, and translate scholarly research into practical business value, and real-world challenges into impactful research agendas. We share knowledge and impact national policy through organisation of public fora, publications, and discussions on news media platforms.

Official Vision Statement

Leading Interdisciplinary Community

To be one of the leading interdisciplinary communities that shape the future of Information Systems in Ghana and Africa by fostering synergistic partnerships between academia and industry, ultimately driving innovation, responsible technological adoption, and a more digitally fluent society.

STRATEGIC PRIORITIES

Core Objectives

The five core pillars guiding the activities, discussions, and public fora of the ISAP Forum.

1

Facilitate Knowledge Exchange

Promote the sharing of research findings, industry best practices, case studies, and practical tools between academics, researchers, and IS practitioners across Ghana and West Africa.

2

Stimulate Collaboration

Encourage joint research projects, guest lectures, mentorship, and industry-sponsored academic initiatives to solve pressing organizational and societal problems.

3

Support Career Development

Provide students, young researchers, and early-career practitioners with access to mentors, networking opportunities, and insights into emerging career pathways in Information Systems.

4

Debate Critical Issues & Shape Policy

Offer a constructive platform for discussing hot topics, emerging trends, ethical concerns, data protection, cybersecurity, and national IT policy implications in Ghana and Africa.

5

Translate Research into Practical Value

Ensure that scholarly research in Information Systems directly informs business practices, public sector digital transformation, and national policy formulation.

WHO WE SERVE

Target Audience & Community Groups

ISAP Forum connects five core stakeholder categories across Ghana and the broader African continent.

01

Academics & Researchers

University lecturers, professors, and researchers seeking empirical validation, industry data, joint research partners, and outlets for scholarly work.

02

Policy Stakeholders & Regulators

Government policy makers and regulatory bodies in Ghana, including key national institutions:

Ministry of Communication & Digitalization National Communications Authority (NCA) National Information Technology Agency (NITA) Data Protection Commission (DPC) Cybersecurity Authority (CSA)
03

IS & IT Practitioners

Information Systems professionals, IT directors, CIOs, enterprise architects, software engineers, and digital consultants applying tech solutions in industry.

04

IS & CS Students

Undergraduate and postgraduate students in Information Systems, Computer Science, and Information Technology seeking mentorship, research guidance, and industry insights.

05

Industry Organizations

Technology companies, public sector bodies, financial institutions, and enterprises seeking evidence-based IS research and emerging academic talent.
